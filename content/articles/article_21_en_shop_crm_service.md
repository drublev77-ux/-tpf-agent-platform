# Online Shop + CRM Integration: orders that reach your CRM without manual work

An online shop without integrations is manual labour: every order gets re-typed into the CRM, statuses are updated by hand, sums are copied into the accounting system. While a human does this, leads get lost, replies lag and errors pile up. We connect your shop to CRM and accounting services through **open APIs** — order, customer and payment data flows automatically, and all you do is check the result.

## What your business gets after integration

1. **Orders reach the CRM on their own.** A customer checks out — a deal card is created automatically with items, total and contact details. No retyping, no missed leads.
2. **Statuses stay in sync.** Order shipped — the CRM status updates and the customer gets an email with the tracking number. Stock changed — the storefront and the inventory show the same thing.
3. **Stock is accurate everywhere.** Inventory is linked to the catalogue: when an item runs out, the Buy button hides itself — no selling air.
4. **Bookkeeping and reports without manual transfer.** Excel exports and imports, daily and weekly summaries, PDF documents in one click.
5. **Customer emails go out by themselves.** Order confirmation, status change, a finished document — no manual mailing.

## How we do it

The work runs in four transparent steps:

1. **Process review.** You describe what is done manually today and in which services. We pin down exactly what data must flow between the shop, CRM and accounting.
2. **Free spec and estimate.** Within an hour you get it by email: what exactly we connect, through which APIs, and what it costs. Free, before any payment.
3. **Integration via open APIs.** The shop links to your CRM (amoCRM, Bitrix24, HubSpot, Pipedrive and others with an open API), accounting services and n8n / Zapier / Make — through webhooks and REST/GraphQL APIs.
4. **Check the demo before paying.** You test the connection on a review version: how an order goes out, how the notification arrives, how the status updates. The second half of the payment comes only after you have checked everything.

## What we connect

* **CRM systems** with an open API: amoCRM, Bitrix24, HubSpot, Pipedrive and others
* **Workflow automation:** n8n, Zapier, Make — requests flow into your scenario by webhook
* **Accounting and sheets:** Excel export and import, catalogue and stock synchronisation
* **Email notifications:** order confirmations, status-change emails, documents to customers
* **Payment systems** (Stripe, bePaid and others) — connected to your own account, money goes straight to you
* **AI inside the process:** a model reads a receipt photo or PDF and fills in the fields, drafts replies — a human confirms the result

## Honest limitations

We do not connect banks directly, government systems, software installed on your computers, or services without an open API. You check every integration on a demo version before final payment. If a service has no open API, we say so honestly before we start, not midway.
